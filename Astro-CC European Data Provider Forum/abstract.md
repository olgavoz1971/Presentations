# A newcomer's experience in publishing photometric time series with DaCHS
*Lightning talk*
**Author:** Olga Vozyakova
*Astro-CC European Data Provider Forum, Heidelberg, Germany, 25-27 March 2026

---

### Abstract
We set out to build a VO resource for variable star researchers by assembling photometric time series from small telescopes scattered across personal computers and institutional archives. We started with our time series and images from the Kolonica Observatory. 

Inspired by this experience, I turned to the OGLE Collection of Variable Stars (OCVS) archive and, with typical newcomer confidence, decided to republish it via TAP, SSAP and the Cone Search on observed objects. 

Only after obtaining permission did I realise the scale: 1.7 billion measurements of 700 thousand objects, distributed across dozens of directories and heterogeneous metadata files. By then, retreat was no longer an option. 

The OCVS archive is now accessible for testing. Any criticisms and suggestions are welcome
